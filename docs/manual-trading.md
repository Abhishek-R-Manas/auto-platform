# Manual real trading · workspace 0.14

The owner requested a real-money trading workflow. Manual NSE delivery authority is now separate from forward-strategy qualification. The original strategy-linked authority still requires its qualified forward evidence and guardian approval. A manual account cannot dispatch strategy or AI entries.

Supported execution is NSE one-unit delivery-equity/ETF contracts, CNC, regular DAY LIMIT orders. Every buy requires a protective trigger. Verified fills create native SL sell orders, with a limit exit if the trigger is already crossed. Sell tickets reduce only reconciled, unreserved stock bought through this app. Existing imported holdings remain available for viewing and charting; selling them is not authorized by this implementation. Futures, options baskets, MIS/MTF, market/AMO orders, order modification, GTT and automatic overnight stop renewal are unavailable. The strategy builder remains analysis and paper research.

## Customer journey

1. **Connect account.** Sign in through Connections, then open Trading setup and select Refresh account. The first stage displays the verified account and cash-only funds, and checks NSE/CNC/LIMIT/SL permissions. Completed account checks remain visible for the same authenticated session and trading date. Their persistence grants no execution authority: a separate 15-second snapshot freshness check still protects every execution action.
2. **Set limits.** Choose specific symbols or All supported NSE stocks and ETFs, then use available broker funds with no app-defined caps, or choose optional custom budgets. Enter reviewer and permission expiry in India time. Each field explains its unit and constraint. No rupee allocation is prefilled. Validate limits highlights missing, invalid or unsupported values. Current master identity, capital/order/loss relationships and expiry are checked server-side.
3. **Broker permissions.** Follow the official links to configure the trading server's static-IP whitelist in Kite Connect Profile. Verify DDPI/POA or applicable daily CDSL authorization in Kite. Keep TPIN/OTP on the broker/depository page. Confirm these external actions explicitly, and authorize native protective exits. The documented read APIs do not expose the developer whitelist, and successful portfolio access does not prove order-write acceptance. The wizard labels these as owner confirmations; it never invents broker verification.
4. **Review and activate.** Refresh & validate readiness obtains current account evidence and checks allocation against cash, account permissions, all confirmations, negative account P&L against the daily halt, stop coverage, unresolved actions, reallocation restrictions and audit integrity. Failed checks stay visible with an action; passed checks can be expanded. Changing inputs invalidates the old review, and inputs are locked while a validation/save is in progress. Preflight creates no authority, activation or entry order. Type the exact permission phrase to save reviewed limits. Saved fields and confirmations rehydrate after reload. Then type ARM REAL MONEY followed by the account ID to activate the session separately; activation refreshes evidence and rechecks account readiness.
5. Open Trade, select Live broker account, select a supported equity and choose Buy. Enter units, limit, protective trigger and rationale. The review displays account, instrument, quantity, notional, estimated charges and planned risk. Type the exact order confirmation and send once. The 20-second preview and every submission recheck authority, quotes, funding and risk.
6. Open Orders to follow acknowledgement, actual fills and protective exits. Sync status reads the broker. An acknowledged order may remain unfilled or be rejected; it is never represented as a completed fill.
7. To reduce stock, cancel the corresponding native protection explicitly and inspect the confirmed cancellation first. Cancelling protection pauses entries. After confirmed cancellation, review a Sell ticket for the verified unreserved units; reductions remain possible while entries are paused. Restore reviewed broker protection if retaining the position.

The execution account selector does not change Portfolio’s tabs. The sidebar count follows the selected Portfolio tab and survives paper-state refreshes: Kite holding count, open broker positions or paper positions. Missing synced broker data shows a dash, rather than a false zero.

## Risk and recovery contract

The shared service commits each durable request identity and reservation before sending to Kite. Duplicate submission of the same preview returns the recorded intention. Timeout/uncertain responses halt entries; the app never retries them automatically. Reconcile by broker ID and exact tag, inspect Kite and resolve the outcome before creating a replacement. A cancellation acknowledgement stays pending and retains quantity reservations until actual broker evidence confirms it.

Manual onboarding defaults to **BROKER_FUNDS**, which imposes no app allocation, single-order value, daily-loss budget, per-trade loss budget or daily-entry ceiling. The five budget fields are stored as null, with an explicit funding-policy enum; no infinity or large-number substitute is used. New orders use actual conservative cash-only broker funds after outstanding commitments and estimated entry charges. This mode does not impose a lifetime gross-purchase ceiling, and it does not infer credit or settlement availability beyond broker evidence.

**CUSTOM** is optional. Its amounts/counts have no app-imposed upper ceiling; they must be positive, finite and consistent with the owner's allocation, and the initial allocation must fit actual cash. Existing saved custom amounts, loss halts and daily counts remain effective until an explicit reviewed reconfiguration. Broker-funded mode has no daily-loss or per-trade-budget halt. Both modes retain native stop-limit protection, current-contract/quote/depth checks, owner confirmations, durable requests, pending reservations, uncertain-action handling and actual-funds validation. Broker/exchange restrictions and final acceptance still apply.

Native stop-limit orders can remain unfilled after gaps, and DAY stops expire at session end; supervise existing positions and protection in Kite. Delivery authorization and static-IP confirmations remain owner assertions until supported by actual broker acceptance. No release validation places a genuine order.

Ordinary NSE sessions are checked against the official [2026 equity circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR71775.pdf) and [NSE trading calendar](https://www.nseindia.com/resources/exchange-communication-holidays?apiversion=v2), reviewed 2026-11-05 through 2026-11-12. Weekends, November 10 and unreviewed special sessions are blocked. New entry review stops at 15:20 IST. Authority cannot extend past the calendar review. Subsequent dates require a fresh server calendar review and release. This is explicit bounded validity, not an assumption that all future weekdays are tradable.

## Connection deployment

`ARTHA_LIVE_TRANSPORT_ENABLED` remains off by default and is never accepted from a browser credential form. Following the owner's request, the validated `tools/enable_manual_transport.py --owner-requested` can install `deploy/artha-live-transport.conf` under the managed service's drop-in directory. It requires the exact tested deployment, healthy audit, no mandate, no broker orders and no active account. It backs up and restores checks all stores, restarts paused, verifies portfolio/paper/watchlist/registry preservation and records the actual connection state. If enablement fails it restores the prior connection configuration.

Enabling the connection does not save a mandate, infer capital, assert broker prerequisites, sign in, arm the session or submit an order. The owner must complete Trading setup. A return to disabled transport with outstanding broker exposure must be handled as an operator incident because it can also prevent protective requests. Supervise broker positions in Kite during recovery.

## Workspace 0.12 validation and deployment

`POST /api/pilot/manual/review` accepts the draft and returns field errors and individual PASS / FAIL / PENDING outcomes. It changes neither the journal, mandate nor arming state and calls no broker write. Manual saving uses the same preflight, so direct clients cannot bypass cash, account permission or daily-loss validation by skipping the UI. Server connection capability remains a separate deployment/activation gate: an otherwise valid authority can be saved while transport is off, but cannot be armed or submitted.

`tests/test_trading_onboarding.py` covers these guarantees, retained progress with expired execution data, session/date changes, failed sync, field-error redaction and activation after account permission/fund changes. `tools/trading_onboarding_browser.py` covers missing fields, external confirmations, daily-loss failure, insufficient funds, editing after validation, explicit save without activation, reload and mobile layout. It cannot enable a new connection or carry forward session activation. It backs up all stores, verifies exact validated source and preserves saved authority, cached portfolio, paper financial state and watchlist. Restart clears session authentication and execution reconciliation by design. No source archive or validation artifact includes private holdings or account data.

## All-stock manual permission · workspace 0.14

Onboarding step 2 has a Stock permission selector. SELECTED requires one to ten explicit symbols. ALL_NSE_EQ requires an empty symbol list and applies only to the manual delivery authority. No wildcard ticker is interpreted. Existing stored mandates without instrument_scope remain selected-symbol permissions; no migration broadens them. Qualified strategy-linked mandates still require explicit qualified bindings and cannot accept the all-stock field.

The all-stock permission covers currently eligible NSE EQ one-unit stocks and ETFs, including eligible new listings during the authority period. Indices, non-unit lots, other exchanges and derivatives are excluded. The current master is checked for every preview, submit and protective-exit contract; stale/missing quotes, mismatched tokens, unsupported instruments and ordinary execution gates still block requests. This permission is not a claim that Zerodha accepts every stock or every price. Broker restrictions, funds, depth and other checks can reject an order.

Capital reservations, maximum order value, daily count, loss thresholds and open risk remain shared across the account, not reset per symbol. All-stock mode checks external pending orders against the requested symbol, outstanding Artha orders and Artha-owned positions, while unrelated stocks outside the managed exposure do not block the entire broad universe. Uncertain Artha actions still block globally. Sell requests still reduce only reconciled, unreserved stock bought through this app; the permission cannot sell unrelated imported holdings. No blanket subscription, bulk quote fetch or automatic investment is triggered.

The review and saved summary name the universe explicitly. A scope change invalidates the prior review and requires an explicit save; numeric limits and broker consents are not inferred or changed. The selector and scope survive reload. `tests/test_all_stocks.py` covers current and newly listed equities, malformed or unsupported contracts, legacy permissions, strategy restrictions, current quotes, shared budgets/counts and owned-stock exits. The guided browser journey covers scope selection, review invalidation, explicit save, persistence and selected-list validation.

Instrument fields checked against [Kite's instrument-master contract](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/market-quotes/) on 2026-11-05. Master last_price is not used as an execution quote.


## Broker-funded trading · workspace 0.14

The owner requested removal of app-defined limits. New manual drafts default to **Use available broker funds · no app-defined caps**. Optional custom budget fields are hidden in this mode, and the review/saved summaries identify unconfigured app limits as unconfigured rather than displaying zero or infinity. Switching funding policy invalidates the old review and requires an explicit save. Saved legacy/custom authorities rehydrate with their own limits preserved; updates cannot silently remove them.

The old ₹1 lakh allocation, ₹25,000/order, ₹10,000 loss-budget and ten-entry ceilings have been removed, as have fixed order-quantity/price schema ceilings. Finite positive prices, whole paise, positive integer quantities, instrument lot/tick identity and broker/exchange constraints remain. Cash plus charges and outstanding reservations is checked before any request; the order connection and account activation are separate from saving this policy. Reviewed session/calendar, authority expiry, quote freshness, protective stops, owned-stock reductions and uncertainty handling still apply.

Qualified strategy authority retains its separate forward allocation and risk-policy gates and cannot request BROKER_FUNDS. `tests/test_broker_funded.py` covers orders and custom amounts above old ceilings, more than ten daily entries, no inferred loss halt, actual-cash/pending-reservation rejection, confirmations, stops, uncertainty, null-budget backup/restart handling and legacy-limit preservation. All executions in tests use a fictional broker.

Broker-funded regular limit orders need valid fresh bid/ask evidence but do not require their entire quantity to be available at the best displayed quote, and do not apply the old 1% app price-deviation/spread filter. The exact owner-confirmed limit price and actual-funds reservation remain binding; Zerodha can leave the order pending, partially fill it, or reject it. Protection is reserved only for broker-verified filled increments. Custom/legacy authority retains its prior depth and deviation checks.
