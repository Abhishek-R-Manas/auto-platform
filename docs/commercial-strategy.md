# Commercial strategy: Centaur Strategy Forge

Owner direction recorded 2026-11-04 during Phase 4 implementation. This is a commercial
positioning and product hypothesis, not evidence of regulatory authorization, trading alpha,
funding commitments, a valuation or measured market leadership.

## Working product position

AI-assisted trading research and supervision with a human guardian. Options architecture
is the initial differentiator: explicit multi-leg construction, volatility/skew, expiry,
liquidity, margin, Greeks, scenarios and loss/execution uncertainty. Stocks, stock swing/
positional/intraday and futures remain in the product; do not silently narrow the platform.
The working name is “Centaur Strategy Forge.” Brand availability has not been established.

AI proposes specifications and explains evidence. Deterministic engines simulate, size and
check risk independently. An authorized human reviews exact immutable versions and evidence.
The user retains visibility and intervention authority. Broker execution is a later separately
authorized and qualified path; Phase 4 has no live order adapter.

“Compliance-first generative wealth infrastructure” is an intended positioning. Describe
current functionality as a paper research/supervision platform, not an already licensed
wealth-management service. “Institutional-grade,” “99% automated,” “optimized,” “unique,”
“alpha,” and premium valuation are unproven claims requiring separate evidence.

## Two roadmaps, one product

| Commercial stage | Goal | Engineering prerequisites and gates |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Sandbox / forge | Paper trading, candidate research, options risk and transparent human review | Existing engineering Phases 1–3 plus Phase 4 prospective evaluation, stress, monitoring and recovery. No live execution. |
| 2 — Community validation / 1K target | Test usefulness, retention, willingness to pay and supported broker journeys | Appropriate entity/activities authorization before regulated services; licensed data use; tenant isolation, identity/roles and complaints/support. Qualified live activity only through engineering Phase 5's explicit pilot gate. “1K” is a target, not traction or proof of alpha. |
| 3 — Institutional readiness / pre-seed / 10K target | Demonstrate repeatable delivery economics and fund responsible scaling | External review, contractual/regulatory readiness, measured service capacity, security and qualified forward/live evidence. Engineering Phase 6 scaling follows evidence. Funding and 10K users are targets, not guaranteed outcomes. |

Formal authorization cannot be deferred simply until the third commercial stage. SEBI's
2026 RA master circular/FAQs describe registration requirements for research entities and
qualification/certification of employed analysts. A qualified reviewer alone does not establish
that every platform activity or entity is authorized. Personalized investment advice can have
a different classification from general research; broker/algo-provider duties are separate.
Obtain a documented classification and release requirements for the actual business flows
before offering applicable services. This document is a planning interpretation, not legal clearance.

## Guardian workflow to preserve

Idea → dated inputs → immutable specification → reproducible historical evaluation → sealed
holdout → prospective paper evidence → authorized human review → bounded user-authorized
execution, when permitted → continuous independent monitoring → intervention/retirement.

Review evidence must eventually identify reviewer authority, qualifications, conflicts,
version, risk policy, permitted users/use, expiry of approval and revocation. The current
single-owner reviewer text field is an operator assertion; it is not an independently verified
license or multi-person authorization system. Multi-user identity, role separation, suitability/
appropriateness as applicable, user consent and customer record obligations are commercial
release work. Never describe the current local workspace as ready for a public 1,000-user beta.

## Data and research expansion

FII/DII flows, macroeconomic releases/revisions, option-chain snapshots and volatility/skew
are requested research inputs, not currently verified production feeds. Each needs an approved
source, usage rights, coverage/latency metrics, original publication and receipt times, revision
history and a point-in-time research contract. Kite price access alone does not establish this
coverage or permission to redistribute data to customers. Source agreements must match the
commercial distribution model. Avoid copying the personal API setup into a multi-user service.

Theta decay is one measurable strategy input, not guaranteed income. Evaluation must include
movement/volatility risk, gaps, short-option losses, liquidity, partial legs, expiry and physical
settlement obligations, after-cost outcomes and uncertainty. Preserve unsuccessful experiments
and evaluate stock/futures alternatives and cash benchmarks as appropriate.

## Evidence for a funding conversation

- Product value: active cohorts, decisions understood, useful interventions, retention and
  paid conversion where lawful; distinguish signups, activated users and funded/trading users.
- Research quality: reproducibility, failed trials, untouched prospective evidence, benchmark
  comparisons, after-cost results, drawdown and uncertainty; no return guarantee.
- Guardian economics: review throughput, time per case, rejection/revision rate, conflicts,
  incident burden and user support cost. Measure automation rather than claiming 99%.
- Operational quality: quote/data coverage, stale-data blocks, reconciliation, execution delay,
  alert delivery, recovery drills, uptime and source/license traceability.
- Business viability: tested pricing, gross margin after data/model/reviewer/support costs,
  acquisition/retention economics and concentration/provider dependency.

## Funding narrative and implemented evidence boundary

Working pitch: **Centaur Strategy Forge is building compliance-first generative wealth
infrastructure for India's mass-affluent investors: an AI options architect, independent
portfolio risk controls and an accountable human guardian.** The present demonstration is a
paper sandbox: declarative multi-leg construction, conservative execution/accounting, option
sensitivity and stress estimates, prospective experiments and expiring evidence-bound human
reviews. Stocks and futures remain supported research alternatives.

The proposed defensibility is the accumulated chain of point-in-time inputs, failed and
successful research, exact strategy versions, guardian decisions and prospective operating
evidence. Validate that thesis with data rights, review economics, paid retention and outcomes;
no measured competitive moat, alpha, automation percentage or premium valuation is claimed.
The forward UI exposes the three commercial stages and their 1,000/10,000-user targets, and
states missing FII/DII, macro and dedicated IV/skew feeds explicitly. No source is fabricated.

Before selling applicable regulated research or a broker-executed black-box algorithm,
resolve the entity's registration, algo-provider/broker arrangements and implementation
requirements. A licensed employee alone does not settle those release requirements. The
[NSE retail-algo FAQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/inline-files/FAQ_Retail%20Algo_03112025_NSE.pdf),
reviewed 2026-11-04, describes RA/algo-provider requirements and broker-hosted vendor strategies;
its referenced circulars control if there is a discrepancy. Read that alongside the
[SEBI RA master circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/master-circulars/feb-2026/master-circular-for-research-analysts_99571.html).
These constraints must be addressed before the relevant community services, rather than
waiting for the fundraising stage. Entity authorization, verified licensing, customer
identity/tenant isolation, commercial data feeds/rights and broker execution are outstanding
commercial release dependencies, not implemented capabilities of this Phase 4 sandbox.
